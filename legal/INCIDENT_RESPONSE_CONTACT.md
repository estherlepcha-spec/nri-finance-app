If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in the NRI's & Expat's Personal Finance Manager, or you suspect your data may have been exposed, contact us:

**Email:** **[security@yourdomain.com]**
(If you have not set up a dedicated security address, use **[privacy@yourdomain.com]**.)

Please include, where possible:
- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce (for vulnerabilities).
- The date/time and any relevant account email (for suspected incidents).
